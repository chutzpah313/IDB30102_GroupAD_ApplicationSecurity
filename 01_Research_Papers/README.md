# Research Papers

This folder contains research paper information and academic sources supporting Chapter 2 of the Research Proposal.

For each important paper, the following information should be recorded:

- Paper title
- Author(s)
- Publication year
- Research problem
- Method or technique
- Dataset or tools used
- Main findings
- Limitations
- Relevance to the proposed research

Copyrighted research papers should not be uploaded unless redistribution is legally permitted. Where necessary, provide the citation, DOI or official article link instead.
